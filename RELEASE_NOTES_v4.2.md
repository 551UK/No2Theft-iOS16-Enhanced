# No2Theft Enhanced iOS16 v4.2

**By 551UK & Elias Sfeir** — For iOS 16 with Dopamine rootless.

### What's new
- **Restored SMS alerts** and the two recipient-number settings for alarm and remote commands.
- **Real GPS locations** in email and SMS alerts, using the iPhone's location rather than IP-based estimates.
- **SOS flashlight** flashes a repeating SOS pattern to help locate a missing phone.
- **Remote command phrases** trigger the alarm and configured SMS, email, photo and SOS features.
- **Photo Crash Guard** reduces SpringBoard crashes caused by legacy camera exceptions.
- Improved Settings layout with all options together, custom PIN, Gmail SMTP, and clear setup guidance.

### Important GPS setup
**OPEN THE NO2THEFT LOCATION APP ON YOUR HOME SCREEN AND ALLOW PRECISE LOCATION FOR ACCURATE GPS IN EMAIL AND SMS ALERTS.**

### Quick Gmail Custom SMTP setup
1. Enable **2-Step Verification** on your Google Account, then generate a 16-character [Google App Password](https://myaccount.google.com/apppasswords).
2. Go to **Settings → No2Theft Enhanced iOS16 → Email settings**, turn on **Send Email**, and enter your recipient address.
3. Under **GMAIL SMTP**, enable **Use Custom SMTP**, enter your Gmail address in **Sender Gmail**, then enter the **app password** (not your normal Google password) in **Gmail App Password**.
4. Tap **Apply Changes**, respring if needed, and send a test alert. If you want remote-command emails too, enable the relevant Remote Commands email switch.

> Security: This tweak stores the app password without encryption. You can revoke it at any time from your Google Account.

Existing PIN, email and alert functionality is retained. The original v1.3.5 release remains available for rollback.
